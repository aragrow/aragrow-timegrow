Mobile Access (a card in Settings) controls the mobile website, for the daily tasks: Clock In / Out or Manual Time Entry (by the person's Time Entry Method), and Record Expenses with receipts. Only team members get a PIN; administrators log in with their password, on a phone too (the "log in with your password" link on the mobile login page).

Each team member can be given time tracking, expenses, the AI voice assistant and AI receipt reading on mobile. They log in at /mobile-login with their username and a 6-character PIN made by TimeGrow (Generate PIN, here or on their profile). The login lasts 8 hours and only opens those pages; the menu has Log out. 5 wrong PINs lock the PIN for 15 minutes (Unlock here).

When the site uses Wordfence Login Security and a team member has two-factor authentication on, the mobile login asks for their authenticator code after the PIN, the same code as the normal login.
