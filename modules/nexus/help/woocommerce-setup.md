TimeGrow invoices through WooCommerce, which is free; it needs no key. Install and activate the WooCommerce plugin first (Plugins > Add New, search "WooCommerce").

Then open Settings > WooCommerce Integration in TimeGrow and turn on what you use: clients (use the clients' WooCommerce billing details), products (each project's hidden, virtual product is made when the project is saved or first billed; without this, no products are made and invoice lines carry the project's name only), and decimal quantities (so 1.5 hours is billed as 1.5, not rounded).

After that, bill time from Process Time: it turns billable time into WooCommerce orders. To send the invoices through PayPal, set up PayPal too.
