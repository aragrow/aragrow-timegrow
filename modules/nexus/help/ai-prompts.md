Settings > AI Prompts keeps dated versions of the instructions the Voice AI, its help answers ("Voice AI: help answers") and the Receipt Reader follow. In the help answers prompt, {agent} stands for the assistant's name. Only one version per tool is active; older ones are archived with their notes, so you can see what changed and go back. A version that leaves out a field TimeGrow needs can't be activated.
