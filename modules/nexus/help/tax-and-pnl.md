The Yearly Tax Report totals a year's expenses by IRS Schedule C category, with invoices and payments, ready for your accountant. The Monthly Profit & Loss Report shows income and expenses month by month on a cash basis: income when payment is received, expenses when paid. Pick the year at the top of each.
