Administrators find every time entry under Work > Time Entries. Click Edit under an entry to change its project, team member, day, hours, billable or description, then save. Every change is recorded in the Time Entry Audit Log with who made it and what changed.

A billed entry is already on an invoice; if its hours change, the invoice does not change by itself.
