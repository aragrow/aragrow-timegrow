The Unbilled Time report lists billable hours that are not on an invoice yet, by client and project, with the amount each would bill at: the project's rate, else the client's, else the company's. Entries older than 30 days show in red, and the cards show the total and how old the oldest entry is.

Use Bill This Time to open Process Time and invoice it. Inactive entries and open clock-ins are not counted.
