Click the round microphone icon in the bottom-right corner of any TimeGrow admin page to open the chat. Tap the mic button in the chat and talk, or type and press Enter. You can tell it what you worked on, punch in or out, ask questions about your data, or ask how to use TimeGrow.

It remembers the conversation while the chat is open; closing it or reloading the page starts over. Nothing is saved until you confirm. The AI conversation needs an AI key set up by an administrator; without it the chat asks each question one at a time.
