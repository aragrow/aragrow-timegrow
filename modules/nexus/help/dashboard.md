The TimeGrow item in the left menu opens the dashboard. The My Time tab shows your hours this week and this month, how much of it is billable, your billable hours not invoiced yet, whether you are clocked in, your hours per day for the last 14 days and your top projects this month. Hover over a bar to see its value, or open "Show as table".

Administrators also get a Team & Business tab: team hours, the billable share, unbilled work in dollars, what was invoiced this month, active projects, team hours per week and hours by team member and by client. Below the tabs, cards open each part of TimeGrow.
