On the Time Entries list, the quick range buttons (Today, This week, Last month, This year and more) set the From and To dates in one click and keep your other filters. Below them, filter by project, client, team member, billable, billed, status and type, add a keyword, and press Filter. Clear Filters resets everything. Click a column title to sort by it.
