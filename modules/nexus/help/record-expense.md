Open Record Expenses from the dashboard. Enter the name, date, amount and description, pick a category and a payment method, and assign it to a project, a client or general. Upload the receipt if you have one, then save.

Categories follow the IRS Schedule C lines, so the yearly tax report can group them. If a receipt is uploaded and the AI is set up, it can fill the fields in for you.
