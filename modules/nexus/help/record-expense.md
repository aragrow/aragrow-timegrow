Open Record Expenses from the dashboard. Add up to five receipts (JPG, PNG or PDF, 512 KB each) if you have them. Choose who it is for: yourself (a general expense), one of your projects or one of your clients. Enter the name, date, amount and category, pick how it was paid, then save. Your latest expenses and their receipts are listed under the form.

Team members record only their own expenses. Administrators can record an expense for any team member, or for the company, and assign it to any project or client.

Categories follow the IRS Schedule C lines, so the yearly tax report can group them. If you may read receipts with AI and it is set up, "Read the first receipt with AI" fills the fields in for you; check them before saving.
