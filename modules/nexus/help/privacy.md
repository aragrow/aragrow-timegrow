Before anything goes to the AI, TimeGrow replaces client and team member names, emails and phone numbers with stand-ins like "Client 31", and puts the real names back only on your screen. Team members only get their own time. The assistant can't delete anything or change many records at once, and nothing is saved until you confirm.
