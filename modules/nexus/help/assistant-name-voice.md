The assistant is called Sam Spade unless an administrator renamed it in Settings > AI Voice Entry > Assistant name. You can call it by name: "Sam, how many hours this week?".

It speaks with the voice chosen in Settings > AI Voice Entry, or the most natural voice your device has. To use another voice on your device, click Voice at the top of the chat; voices marked with a star sound the most natural. Sound on / Sound off turns speech off, and your choice is remembered on that device.
