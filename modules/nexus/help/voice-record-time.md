Say what you did in one sentence, for example "Two hours yesterday on the SEO audit for Copperleaf, keyword research." The assistant fills in the client, project, day, hours, billable and description, and only asks for what is missing. Because you give the hours, it does not ask for a start time; say one ("from 9 to 11") if you want it saved.

When everything is there it reads back a summary. Say "save it" or tap Save entry. You can correct anything first: "actually three hours" or "no, the website project".
