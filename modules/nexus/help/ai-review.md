Settings > AI Conversations lists saved voice assistant conversations and receipt readings. Open one to see what was said and how the AI read it, rate it from 1 to 5 stars with a note, and mark each AI reading thumbs up or down. Old conversations are removed after the number of days set in the voice or receipt settings.
