Open Manual Time Entry from the dashboard to add time you already worked. Pick the project, the day and the hours, say whether it is billable, describe the work and save. Use it for time you forgot to clock, or work done away from the computer.

You only see projects you are assigned to (administrators see all projects). The voice assistant can fill this in for you from one sentence.
