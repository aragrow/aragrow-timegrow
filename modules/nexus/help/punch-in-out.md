Open Clock In / Out from the dashboard, pick the project and press the button to clock in; press it again to clock out. The time between counts as your hours.

You can also tell the assistant "punch in" or "clock me in on the SEO audit". It asks for the project if needed and what time you started (say "now" or a time like 9:30), then confirms. "Punch out" asks when you stopped. It won't clock you in twice, out when you are not clocked in, or use a time in the future.

A clock-out must be after its clock-in. TimeGrow refuses a clock-out for an entry that is already clocked out or already billed, from the page, the assistant or the mobile site. The same before/after check applies when an administrator edits a clocked entry.
