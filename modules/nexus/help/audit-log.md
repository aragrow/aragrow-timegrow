The Time Entry Audit Log records every change to a time entry: created, edited, clocked out, billed, unbilled, rebilled and status changes. Each line shows when, who made it, from where (Web, Voice, Mobile or System) and what changed, like "hours 2 → 3.5". Click an entry number to see that entry's whole history. Changes made before the log was installed were not recorded.
