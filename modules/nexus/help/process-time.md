Process Time turns billable, unbilled time into WooCommerce invoices. Pick the client and the dates, review the entries, and process them: TimeGrow creates an order per client with a line per project, priced at the project's, client's or company's rate, and marks the entries as billed. Non-billable and inactive entries are left out.
