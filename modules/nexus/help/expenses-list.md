Administrators see every expense under Work > Expenses. Use the quick ranges or the From and To dates, the type and status filters and a keyword to find expenses, and click Edit to change one.

To leave an expense out of every report without deleting it, set its Status to Inactive. Inactive expenses stay in the list and can be made active again.
