Open Reports from the menu or the dashboard. Everyone has My Hours by Project, My Detailed Time Log and My Expenses Report. Administrators also have Team Hours Summary, Unbilled Time, Client Activity, All Expenses Overview, Time Entry Audit Log, Yearly Tax Report and Monthly Profit & Loss.

Most reports have quick range buttons (Today, Last week, This month, Last year and more) and From and To dates. Export to CSV, or use Print to PDF.
