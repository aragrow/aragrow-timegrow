You can ask questions like "How many hours did I work this week?", "What did I log last?", "Which projects did I work on last month?" or "Am I clocked in?". Follow-ups work too: "and last week?".

Team members only get their own time. Administrators can also ask about the whole team, unbilled hours, unpaid invoices, how much was billed and expenses. The assistant looks at the last 3 months of time and invoices; for older periods it says so and links the reports, which cover any period.
