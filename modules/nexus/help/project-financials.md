The Project Financials report shows, for each project, the hours worked and their value (billable hours at the rate invoices use), how much of that is billed and not billed yet, the expenses assigned to the project, and the net (value minus expenses).

Budget Used compares all hours ever logged on a project with its hour estimate: orange from 90%, red when over. Set a project's estimate in Setup > Projects. Pick a period with the quick ranges, filter by client, and sort by value, net, hours, not billed or budget.
