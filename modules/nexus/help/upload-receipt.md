When you add an expense, upload a photo or PDF of the receipt. If an administrator has set up AI for receipts, TimeGrow reads it and fills in the vendor, date, amount, description and category. Always check the fields before saving: nothing is saved until you press save.

Receipts are private: they are stored with a random name in a protected folder, and open only through the View button on the expense, for people allowed to see expenses. It flags refunds and receipts it can't read clearly. Receipt reading needs your own AI key (Settings > AI); without it you type the fields in yourself.
