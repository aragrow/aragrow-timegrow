Projects are under Setup > Projects. Each project belongs to one client and has a name, an hourly rate, whether it is billable and, optionally, an estimate of hours. Team members only see the projects they are assigned to, unless Settings allows everyone on all projects.

Administrators can also say "add a project for Copperleaf called SEO audit" to the voice assistant. The hourly rate used on invoices is the project's, else the client's, else the company's.
