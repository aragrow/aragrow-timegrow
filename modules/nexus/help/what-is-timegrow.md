TimeGrow is a free WordPress plugin for keeping track of time and expenses. You record the hours you work on each client's projects, record expenses with their receipts, and run reports. Administrators also turn billable time into WooCommerce invoices and PayPal invoices, and track payments.

It is not a full time-tracking platform: there is no payroll, scheduling or resource planning. The AI features (reading receipts and the voice assistant) need your own AI key from OpenAI, Anthropic or Google; everything else works without one.
