TimeGrow > User Guide has every help topic for your role in one page: recording time and expenses, the voice assistant, reports, invoicing and settings. Search it with a word like "receipt", jump to a section, and use "Open this page" to go straight to the screen a topic is about. Settings > Need Help? links to it too.

You can also ask the voice assistant: tap the microphone icon or "Ask the AI assistant" and say "how do I…".
