---
title: Deactivate a time entry
roles: admin
page: timegrow-time-entries-list
keywords: deactivate, inactive, status, remove, hide, delete, time entry, active
---
To take a time entry out of everything without deleting it, open it from Time Entries, set Status to Inactive and save. Inactive entries stay in the list but are left out of reports, the dashboard, invoicing, unbilled time and the voice assistant. Set it back to Active at any time.

A billed entry can't be made inactive; remove it from its invoice first. Use the Status filter on the Time Entries list to see only active or only inactive entries.
