To set up TimeGrow, go in this order. 1) Settings > General: your timezone, currency, and whether team members can record time on every project or only their assigned ones. 2) Setup > Companies: your company and its default hourly rate. 3) Setup > Team Members: link each person to their WordPress user. 4) Setup > Clients: add your clients with their billing email. 5) Setup > Projects: one per piece of work, with its client, rate, billable or not, an hour estimate, and the team members on it.

That is enough to record time and run reports. Optional next steps: set up WooCommerce to invoice, PayPal to send invoices and get paid, an AI key for the voice assistant and receipt reading (Settings > AI), and Mobile Access for phones.
